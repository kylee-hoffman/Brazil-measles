Data dictionary for cleaned dataset

Municipality/year identification:
"muni_code": 7 digit municipality code (numeric)
"muni_name_clean": cleaned all uppercase municipality (character)
"year": year (numeric)

Vaccination:
"MMR2_coverage": MMR vaccine second dose coverage; 1996-2021 (numeric)
"MMR2_goal": Binary: 1 if MMR second dose coverage equal to or greater than goal of 95%, 0 otherwise; 1996-2021 (numeric)
"DTP_coverage": DTP vaccine coverage; 1996-2021 (numeric)
"DTP_goal": Binary: 1 if DTP coverage equal to or greater than goal of 95%, 0 otherwise; 1996-2021 (numeric)

Measles cases:
"measles_cases": number of measles cases for residents of a municipality; 2001-2023 (numeric)

Infectious disease mortality (in progress):
"measles_deaths": number of measles deaths for residents of a municipality; 1996-2023 (numeric)
"mumps_deaths": number of mumps deaths for residents of a municipality; 1996-2023 (numeric)
"whooping_deaths": number of whooping cough deaths for residents of a municipality; 1996-2023 (numeric)

Demographic variables:
"pct_urban_1991": Percent of municipality that lives in an urban area in 1991 (numeric)
"pct_urban_2000": Percent of municipality that lives in an urban area in 2000 (numeric)
"pct_urban_2010": Percent of municipality that lives in an urban area in 2010 (numeric)

"pct_low_inc_1991": percent of municipality with a monthly per capita household income of up to half the minimum wage in 1991 (numeric)
"pct_low_inc_2000": percent of municipality with a monthly per capita household income of up to half the minimum wage in 2000 (numeric)
"pct_low_inc_2010": percent of municipality with a monthly per capita household income of up to half the minimum wage in 2010 (numeric)

"educ_pct_8_yrs_1991": percent of municipality that completed 8 years and more of study in 1991 (numeric)
"educ_pct_8_yrs_2000": percent of municipality that completed 8 years and more of study in 2000 (numeric)
"pct_complete_educ_2010": percent of municipality that completed 2nd cycle of basic education or more in 2010 (numeric)

"population": popualtion of municipality; 1996-2021 (numeric)
"CBR": Live births per 1,000 population; 1996-2021 (numeric)
"IMR": infant mortality rate; number of deaths per 1,000 live births of children under 1 year old; 1996-2021 (numeric)
"CDR": crude death rate; deaths per 1,000 population; 1996-2021 (numeric)
"MHDI": Brazilian Municipal Human Development Index in 1991, 2000, and 2010. three dimensions: education, longevity, and income; 1996-2021 (numeric)
"GINI": Value of Gini coefficient; 1996-2021 (numeric)
"GDP_PC": per capita Gross Domestic Product, current price (BRL 1,00); 1996-2021 (numeric)
"sanitation": Percent of people in households with an inadequate water supply and sanitation; 1996-2021 (numeric)

# Geographic variables:
"muni_code_6": 6-digit municipality code (numeric)
"muni_name": municipality name (character)
"state_name": state name of municipality (character)
"state_code": state code  of municipality (numeric)
"region": region of municipality (character)
